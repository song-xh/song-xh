## About Me

I am a graduate student working at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, decision making, and distributed systems**. My current work focuses on building learning and optimization methods for multi-agent coordination and privacy-aware task allocation.

- **Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning:** heterogeneous cooperation, coordination, re-specialization, CTDE, and policy optimization.
- **Spatial Crowdsourcing:** task assignment, cross-platform collaboration, auction mechanisms, and sequential decision making.
- **Large Language Models:** LLM agents, planning, reasoning, and decision support.
- **Federated Learning:** privacy-preserving cross-platform learning and knowledge aggregation.
- **Optimization & Simulation:** combinatorial optimization, scheduling, graph-based modeling, and simulation environments.
